# Changelog

Release notes as published on the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6772468615).

## 1.3.2 (November 2026)

- Photos keep their capture date, camera details, location, and copyright when you compress, convert, resize, crop, rotate, or watermark them.
- AI Generation: the default 8-bit model now installs completely, and interrupted downloads continue where they stopped.
- AI Anonymize hides content just as well on very large photos, Solid Color is fully opaque by default, and object names appear in your language. Saved images leave out location and identifying details.
- AI OCR keeps the pages it has already read when you stop it or a page fails, reads rotated PDF pages in full, and tells you when a PDF is password-protected.
- Folder Automation retries files after a temporary problem, stops after the current file when paused, and picks up again when an external drive is reconnected. Shortcuts and AppleScript report clearer errors, and AppleScript gains a stop command.
- Images come out upright and exactly sized after rotating, cropping, and converting, transparency survives AI Upscale and AI Eraser, and images too large to process get a clear message.

## 1.3.1 (September 2026)

- AI Anonymize adds three styles: Hex Mosaic, Solid Color (with your own color and opacity), and Rainbow Blocks.
- Changes to Artist, Copyright, Description, and Date Taken in Metadata now save reliably, including on macOS 27. Metadata also shows the IPTC Creator.
- The Privacy preset in Clear Metadata now also removes the camera owner, lens serial number, and the creator, contact, copyright, and location details stored in IPTC.
- If a model can’t be deleted, Settings now tells you why.
- AI Generation now shows the correct download size for each model.

## 1.3.0 (August 2026)

- Every AI workspace has been redesigned with clearer, responsive controls and complete model and quality guidance.
- Model downloads now share progress between feature pages and Settings, with pause, resume, cancel, and reliable continuation from partial downloads.
- AI Image Generation now puts model and precision choices beside the prompt and completes every required component before generating.
- Improved first-run AI setup, download-source switching, and model storage reporting.

## 1.2.3 (July 2026)

- Tool cards can once again be dragged into the Processing Pipeline.
- The “Add Step” menu now correctly shows operation names in your selected app language.

## 1.2.2 (July 2026)

- A new OtterPix menu bar icon keeps your workflows close at hand. Drop images or folders onto it to send them straight to the Pipeline, and reopen the main window with one click. When the icon is enabled, closing the main window leaves OtterPix available from the menu bar.
- New in Pro: Folder Automation watches selected folders and automatically applies a saved Pipeline preset to new images.
- Run supported tools and saved presets from Shortcuts and AppleScript. Image processing stays on your Mac.
- AI tools now check hardware compatibility before loading models and clearly explain when native Apple silicon is required. This release also improves the reliability of menu bar launch, folder permissions, window handling, accessibility, and localization.

## 1.2.1 (July 2026)

- Stability and polish refinements throughout the app.

## 1.2.0 (July 2026)

- OtterPix now speaks 18 languages — we've added Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Vietnamese, French, Italian, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Swedish, Finnish, Norwegian, Turkish, Russian, and Ukrainian. Every menu, tool, and message is fully localized.
- Plus stability and polish improvements throughout.

## 1.1.4 (July 2026)

- AI Text Recognition: more faithful output — one-word headings and lead-in lines above figures are no longer dropped, merged table cells align correctly, and figure-only pages aren't misreported as blank. Copying from the rendered view now pastes a self-contained document, with figures included.
- Memory returns to normal after using AI features — both when you leave the page and right after a run finishes.
- Free plan: revisiting a photo you already used today's free run on keeps it free to refine (browsing other photos no longer locks it).
- When a model isn't downloaded yet, the error now offers a one-click Open Settings path in every AI tool.
- ⌘S saves everywhere it should, downloads verify integrity before landing on disk, cancelling a download is no longer shown as an error, and typing in the QR tool is smoother.
