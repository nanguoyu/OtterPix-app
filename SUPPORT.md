# Getting support for OtterPix

Pick the row that matches your situation:

| You need… | Go to |
|---|---|
| Report a bug | [Open an issue](https://github.com/nanguoyu/OtterPix-app/issues/new/choose) |
| Ask "how do I…?" | [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/nanguoyu/OtterPix-app/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| Suggest a feature | [Feature request](https://github.com/nanguoyu/OtterPix-app/issues/new/choose) (concrete requests) or [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/nanguoyu/OtterPix-app/discussions/categories/ideas) (early ideas) |
| Read the guides | [Documentation](README.md#documentation) — English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Deutsch |
| A refund | [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) |
| Manage or cancel a subscription | [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) |
| Report a security issue | [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) — privately, please |
| Anything private or sensitive | hello@otterpix.app |

**Purchases, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple, not by the developer.** OtterPix ships through the Mac App Store, so Apple processes every payment; the developer cannot see your payment details, issue refunds, or cancel subscriptions on your behalf. Refunds go through [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com); subscriptions are managed in your [Apple Account subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions).

Everything else — bugs, questions, ideas, translation fixes — belongs in this repo, where answers are public and searchable. If you'd rather not post something publicly, email hello@otterpix.app.
